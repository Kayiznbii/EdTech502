Process

Researching Service Dogs and Their Roles

Completing this WebQuest requires you to immerse yourself in the world of service dogs and their remarkable contributions to society. Follow these steps to explore, analyze, and create your final project.

Step One - Learn About Service Dogs

Start by learnin about the different types of service dogs and the roles they play in the lives of different individuals. Some examples include:

  • Guide Dogs
  • Hearing Dogs
  • Medical Alert
  • Psychiatric Service Dogs

Step Two - Understand ADA Requirements

The Americans with Disabilities Act outlines the rights afforded to service dog handlersand the responsibilities of the public in providing appropriate accomodations.

  • Rights of Service Dog Handlers
  • Responsibilities of Businesses and Organizations
  • Exemptions and Limitations

Step Three - Explore How to Obtain a Service Dog

Explore the different ways individuals obtain service dogs and service dog prospects.

  • Obtaining through a service dog program.
  • Owner training a service dog.
  • Adoption of trained dogs.

Step Four - Create Your Project

After conducting your research, summarize your findings in a detailed report or presentation. Include:

  • A description of the different types of service dogs and the disabilities they assist with.
  • Key points from the ADA regarding service dog access and public accommodations.
  • Information about the different methods of obtaining a service dog, including training through programs or training an owner’s dog.

Your Project Should

  • Show a thorough understanding of the service dog’s journey.
  • Capture the emotional and practical aspects of their role.
  • Be creative, engaging, and well-informed.

Step Five - Reflect and Share

  • What surprised you most about the world of service dogs?
  • How do ADA requirements protect the rights of service dog handlers?
  • What are the challenges that individuals face when obtaining a service dog through a program versus owner training?

Finally, be prepared to share your work with the class or your peers to spread awareness about these incredible animals and the vital roles they play. Now that you have your roadmap, it’s time to dive into the process. Let’s get started!
