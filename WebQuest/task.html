Task

Understanding Service Dogs

Your task in this WebQuest is to step into the world of service dogs and experience their journey from early training to working alongside a handler. You will learn what they do, how they are trained, and how they are obtained. You will conduct research on various aspects of service dogs, including types of service dogs, requirements set forth by the Americans With Disabilities Act, and the process of obtaining a service dog.

Research Objectives

  • Types of Service Dogs
    • Research the different types of service dogs, including guide dogs, hearing dogs, medical alert dogs, and psychiatric service dogs.
    • Understand the different roles each type of service dog plays and how they assist their people with their disabilities.
  • ADA Requirements
    • Learn about the legal requirements outlined by the Americans with Disabilities Act regarding service dogs.
  • Obtaining A Service Dog
    • Explore the different ways individuals can obtain a service dog, whether through a program, or owner training.
    • Understand the process, challenges, and responsibilities that come along with owner training a service dog.
    • Research the role of service dog organizations and costs associated with both owner training and getting a program trained dog.

Task

  1. Step One - Research
    • Spend time researching the different types of service dogs, how they are obtained, the tasks they are trained to perform, and how they support their people.
    • Look into the ADA guidelines for service dogs and what public access rights are granted for people with disabilities.
  2. Step Two - Create a Summary
    • After your initial research, summarize your findings and write a detailed report.
    • Be sure to include the different types of service dogs and their roles, ADA requirements, and the process for obtaining a service dog.
  3. Step Three - Reflection
    • Reflect on what you have learned, consider the role of service dogs and how they impact the daily lives of individuals. Also consider any challenges for handlers and trainers.
