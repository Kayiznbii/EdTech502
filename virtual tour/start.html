Dogs of the World: Exploring Breeds and their Origins

Welcome

Welcome to the "Dogs of the World" virtual field trip! Have you ever wondered why some dogs are perfectly suited for pulling sleds in the Arctic, while others are ideal companions for city living? On this journey, we'll explore different dog breeds from different parts of the world and learn how their unique traits, histories, and roles have been shaped by their environments.

This field trip is perfect for dog lovers, history buffs, and curious learners alike! Together we will travel across continets, visiting multiple different countaries and continents to see different canine companions. Through pictures, videos, and stories you will discover how these breeds became the amazing dogs we know today!

Instructions

  1. Visit Each Stop: Explore the different destinations on this tour by clicking the different breed names. Each stop features:
    • A brief history of the breed.
    • Photos and videos.
    • Fun facts about how these dogs served their communities and became beloved pets.
  2. Engage with Activities: After exploring each stop, visit our resource center for engaging activities!

Why take This Virtual Tour

Dogs have been humanity's loyal companions for thousands of years, each breed adapting to specific needs and environments. By Understanding their histories and traits, we can appreciate the incredible diversity and contributions of these furry friends.

Throughout this trip, you will not only learn about breeds but also their cultural significance, their unique abilities, and the joy they give to people world wide!

How Long Will It Take

This tour should take about 20-30 minutes to complete, depending on how much time you spend exploring and completing each activity.
